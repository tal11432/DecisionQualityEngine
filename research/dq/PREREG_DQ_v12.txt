DQ-v12 PRE-REGISTRATION: calibrating the absolute floor of the verdict rule from data. Written 2026-11-15T11:41:31Z, before computing anything for it.
Rule being calibrated: "pass clearly better" needs 0.7*best_pass - shot >= FLOOR; "shot clearly better" needs shot - 1.3*best_pass >= FLOOR; otherwise "no clear difference". FLOOR was set by judgement to 0.02 xG; it is now estimated.
Data: the DQ-v11 decision set (shots and ground passes at x >= 90, 360 competitions + Copa America 2024 shots; test competitions Euro 2024, Bundesliga 2023/24, MLS 2023, Copa America 2024), rebuilt by fetch_v11.py + eval_v11.py (frozen models).
(A) Noise criterion (primary): for a random sample of 300 test SHOTS (seed 0) with at least one admissible receiver, 150 perturbations of the situation: every visible player (shooter, teammates, opponents, keeper) moved by N(0, 1 m) in x and y (about the precision of a freeze-frame / broadcast position),
 the pass-value scale ~ LogNormal(0, 0.15) (about the +-30% band at 2 sd) and the shot-value scale ~ LogNormal(0, 0.10) (the xG calibration slope of 0.9). For each shot with a clear verdict at FLOOR = 0 in the unperturbed situation, stability = share of perturbations that keep the same verdict class.
 Conservative margin c = 0.7*best_pass - shot (pass verdicts) or shot - 1.3*best_pass (shot verdicts). Bins of c: [0,0.005), [0.005,0.01), [0.01,0.02), [0.02,0.03), [0.03,0.05), [0.05,inf).
 FLOOR* = lower edge of the first bin whose mean stability is >= 0.90 and stays >= 0.90 in every higher bin; if no bin reaches 0.90, FLOOR* = 0.05 (largest bin edge) and this is reported as "not reached".
(B) Outcome check (supporting): on test PASSES, the same classes with FLOOR in {0, 0.005, 0.01, 0.02, 0.03, 0.05}; for each FLOOR the realised chain value (xG of team shots within 10 s) of "pass clearly better" minus "shot clearly better" passes, with a match-bootstrap 95% CI and counts.
 Expectation stated in advance: the difference grows with FLOOR (cleaner groups); if it does not, the floor has no outcome support and the noise-based FLOOR* stands alone.
The calibrated FLOOR* replaces 0.02 in research/cases/match_review.py and the two match reviews are rerun. Limits: the perturbation sizes are assumptions (stated above) and the floor is absolute, so it ignores that noise grows with the value level (the ratio rule handles part of that).
