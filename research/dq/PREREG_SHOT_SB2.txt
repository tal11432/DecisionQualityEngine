Replication criteria (men, pooled and per competition where n >= 150 shots):
 R1 xG calibration on the new men's shots: calibration slope in [0.8, 1.2] and mean predicted / observed goals in [0.85, 1.15] (xG is the base of both sides of the comparison).
 R2 the ordering of the share with a better pass by shot distance, <12 m < 12-20 m < >20 m, holds in the pooled men's data and in every competition with n >= 150 shots.
 R3 pooled men's shares by band lie within +-7 percentage points of the first run (43.5%, 72.1%, 90.3%); the overall share within +-5 points of 72.9%.
 R4 sensitivity (+1 defender in receiver triangle) reduces the median gap by 30-70% in every competition, as in the first run (-50%).
Also reported: shots, share better, per competition and per band; women's results alongside, as a test of generalisation of the men's-trained models.
