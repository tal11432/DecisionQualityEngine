DQ-v10 PRE-REGISTRATION: the reverse case, "he passed when he could have shot: was it the right call?". Written 2026-11-15T06:08:24Z, before downloading or computing anything for it.
Data: StatsBomb 360 competitions (events + 360 frames), gzip in memory, tables deleted afterwards. All models are FROZEN and already fitted on non-test competitions: shot value Vs (DQ-v8, fitted on shots taken in 360 competitions), chain value xA_chain (DQ-v6), xP hazard (DQ-v2).
 Evaluation on TEST competitions (Euro 2024, Bundesliga 2023/24, MLS 2023); the other 360 competitions are reported separately as "in-sample" only.
Population: open-play ground passes (no restart type, not cross / through ball / header) whose passer is at x >= 90 (StatsBomb units), with a 360 frame, a visible keeper; completed or not. The target of a failed pass is taken as the point where the ball stopped (stated simplification).
"Could have shot": the model value of a shot from the passer's context (Vs_hat, location, keeper, defenders in the shooting triangle, nearby defenders) is >= 0.05; sensitivity 0.10. Vs_hat is trained on shots actually taken, so it is optimistic for contexts where players almost never shoot (stated).
"Was the pass right?" Ex-ante value of the pass taken = xP(path to the target) x xA_chain(target, from the 360 frame at pass time). Compare with Vs_hat with the same +-30% band as before:
 pass clearly better if 0.7 * E_pass > Vs_hat; shot clearly better if 1.3 * E_pass < Vs_hat; otherwise ambiguous. Also compared against the BEST pass available (all visible teammates 3-40 m, not offside by the position proxy): "no pass was clearly better than shooting" = 0.7 * best_EPV < Vs_hat... precisely: the shot is clearly better than every available pass if 1.3 * best_EPV < Vs_hat.
Validity check (the use for improving the model; outcome never enters the ex-ante values): (1) known groups: the REALISED chain value (xG of team shots within 10 s after the pass) of passes classed "shot clearly better" should be lower than that of "pass clearly better" (match-bootstrap 95% CI of the difference excluding 0 in the expected direction).
 (2) calibration where it matters: among could-shoot passes, realised mean chain / mean ex-ante E_pass in [0.7, 1.3]; reported by distance band.
Reported: counts (could-shoot moments, share of all passes from x >= 90), three-way verdict overall, by distance band, completed vs failed, vs the best alternative; the share of "passes where a shot was clearly better" (the mistake candidates) and the realised value they produced vs what the shot would have been worth.
Limits stated in advance: selection (players pass when they see a reason: pass-and-shot value of the unseen situation: body orientation, blocked lane, teammate better placed); the shot value of a context where nobody shoots is an extrapolation; 10-s chain horizon; visible players only; failed-pass targets approximated.
