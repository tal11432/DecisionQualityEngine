DQ-v7 PRE-REGISTRATION: (1) how much does the level of the pass value vary between competitions, and how robust is the shot-vs-pass conclusion across that range; (2) cost of a failed pass.
Written 2026-11-14T17:07:00Z, before downloading or computing anything for it. Data, splits, models, features and gates of DQ-v6 are unchanged (PREREG_DQ_v6.txt); the 360-frame competitions plus Copa America 2024 are re-downloaded in memory (reduced tables deleted afterwards).
(1) Level variability. For every competition with >= 150 test-eligible passes (all 360 competitions, train and test), refit xA_chain on all OTHER competitions and compute r_c = observed chain value / predicted.
 tau = sqrt(max(0, var(log r_c) - mean(1/(positives_c)))) (approximate removal of Poisson noise of the positive count). Report r_c per competition, tau and the 95% range exp(+-1.96 tau).
 Robustness curve: share of test foot shots with pass value > shot value when the chain value is multiplied by s in {0.6, 0.7, 0.8, 0.9, 1.0, 1.1, 1.2, 1.3, 1.4}. Three-way classification per shot with s in [0.7, 1.3] (frozen BEFORE seeing tau):
 "pass clearly better" if pass_chain*0.7 > shot value; "shot clearly better" if pass_chain*1.3 < shot value; otherwise "ambiguous". Report the three shares overall, by distance band and by competition, with match-bootstrap 95% CIs.
 If tau turns out > 0.3, the +-30% band is too narrow and a second classification with s in [exp(-1.96 tau), exp(+1.96 tau)] is added (stated now, to avoid choosing after the fact).
(2) Failure cost. Incomplete open-play ground passes (no restart type, not cross / through ball / header) with end location (where the ball was stopped) in ALL positions. Value of a failure vf = sum of xG of the OPPONENT's open-play shots within 10 s after the pass.
 Model: fractional logistic of vf on the location seen from the opponent's goal (basis dist/angle/x/dist^2/angle*dist, with x' = 120 - x, y' = 80 - y), fit on TRAIN competitions, evaluated on TEST (Euro 2024, Bundesliga 2023/24, MLS 2023, Copa America 2024):
 gate G-vf: pooled predicted/observed in [0.85, 1.15] (CI reported; positives are rare, so a wide CI is expected) and log-likelihood gain over the constant with match-bootstrap CI excluding 0.
 Net pass value for a hypothetical pass to teammate j: xP_j * xA_chain,j - (1 - xP_j) * vf(target_j)   [failure location approximated by the target: stated simplification].
 Compare the shot-vs-pass share with and without the failure cost. Expectation stated in advance: the effect is small in the attacking third because opponent chances after a loss there are rare.
Israeli frames recomputed with the three-way classification. Limits stated in advance: 360 frames omit invisible players; the failure location is approximated; tournaments dominate the sample.
