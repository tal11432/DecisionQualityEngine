DQ-v11 PRE-REGISTRATION: one numeric value for every decision (shot or pass) in the attacking area, with one pooled validation. Written 2026-11-15T07:23:59Z, before downloading or computing anything for it.
Unit: expected goals (xG) of the possession within 10 s (the chain value of DQ-v6/v8), so 0.01 = one hundredth of a goal. All models are FROZEN and fitted on non-test competitions: shot value Vs (DQ-v8), xA_chain (DQ-v6), xP hazard (DQ-v2).
Population: open-play decisions of the player on the ball at x >= 90 (StatsBomb units) in the 360 competitions plus Copa America 2024 (shots only): (a) foot shots, (b) ground passes (no restart, not cross / through ball / header), completed or not. Test competitions (primary): Euro 2024, Bundesliga 2023/24, MLS 2023 (+ Copa America 2024 shots); the rest is in-sample and reported separately.
Per-decision numbers, for a pass-value scale s in {0.7, 1.0, 1.3} (shot values are not scaled):
 V_chosen(s) = Vs_hat of the passer's/shooter's context for a shot;  s * xP * xA_chain at the target for a pass (target of a failed pass = where the ball stopped; flagged).
 V_best(s)   = max( Vs_hat, s * best EPV over the visible teammates 3-40 m not offside by the position proxy, V_chosen(s) ).
 regret(s)   = V_best(s) - V_chosen(s) >= 0;   efficiency(s) = V_chosen(s) / V_best(s) in [0, 1];   the reported number is regret(1.0), with range [min_s, max_s] of regret(s).
 Verdict: "clearly suboptimal" if min_s regret(s) >= 0.01; "near-optimal" if max_s regret(s) < 0.01; otherwise "ambiguous". (0.01 is fixed now; it is about one tenth of the mean shot value in the area.)
 Shot value for BOTH kinds of moment is the same function Vs (context only), so numbers are comparable; for shots the detailed xG (with first-time, volley, ...) is stored as a reference column only.
Pooled validity checks (outcomes never enter the values): (1) known groups on passes: realised chain value of "near-optimal" passes minus "clearly suboptimal" passes, match-bootstrap CI excluding 0 in the expected direction;
 (2) calibration of V_chosen for passes that were completed: realised/ex-ante in [0.8, 1.2]; (3) for shots: mean V_chosen vs mean statsbomb xG + continuation of the same shots within +-15%.
Reported: distribution of regret and efficiency by decision type (shot / pass) and distance band; verdict shares; the 25 largest regrets and 25 smallest, as examples; CSV of every test decision with: match id, period, clock, team, decision type, outcome (completed / failed / goal etc.), V_chosen, best alternative and its type, regret and range, verdict. No player names are written to any output (player-level aggregation is out of scope until validated).
Limits stated in advance: the values describe expected xG from positions, not the quality of the player; failed-pass targets are approximated (verdicts for failed passes favour the pass); shot value is extrapolated where nobody shoots; visible players only; 10-s horizon.
