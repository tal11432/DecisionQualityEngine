DQ-v13 PRE-REGISTRATION: Decision Score v1 (0-100) for on-ball decisions in the attacking third. Written 2026-11-15T12:21:07Z, before any data was downloaded or any new model fitted for it.
Plan: /root/.claude/plans/reactive-tinkering-creek.md (approved by the user). Scope chosen by the user: attacking third; shot, pass, carry/dribble. Zero-anchor chosen by the user: not a constant turnover but a turnover whose danger depends on the situation.

1. MOMENT. Open-play, the player on the ball, ball at x >= 80 (StatsBomb units, 120 x 80, attacking to x = 120), 360 frame at the action (shots: the shot freeze frame), keeper visible. Horizon H = 10 s. Unit: expected goals (xG) of the possession.
   Splits as before: TEST = Euro 2024, Bundesliga 2023/24, MLS 2023 (+ Copa America 2024 for shots); all other competitions train. Test competitions never enter any training set.
2. OPTIONS AND THEIR VALUE Q (net xG in the next 10 s).
   shot:      Q = xG_geo + continuation (frozen DQ-v5 / DQ-v6 models).
   pass j:    ground pass to every visible teammate 3-40 m away, not certainly offside (DQ-v10/v12 rule: certain onside needs a 1 m margin; a hidden defender can only help the attacker):  Q = xP_j * xA_chain_j - (1 - xP_j) * L(target_j).
   carry c:   the ball carried 3, 6 or 10 m in 8 directions (45 degree steps), destination inside the pitch:  Q = P_keep * V_pos(destination) - (1 - P_keep) * L(destination).  Hypothetical option, trained on observed carries.
   dribble d: take on the nearest opponent within 5 m inside +-45 degrees of the direction to goal:  Q = P_dribble * V_pos(2 m beyond that opponent toward goal) - (1 - P_dribble) * L(current position).
   L(location, context) = danger of losing the ball there = E[opponent xG within 10 s] - E[own xG within 10 s after the loss] (two fractional-logistic models, opponent-perspective location basis plus 360 context: opponents ahead of the ball, teammates covering behind, opponents and teammates within 10 units).
   Not in v1 (they need their own completion models): through balls, crosses, lofted passes, clearances, long balls from deep, back-passes as a separate class (a ground pass to a visible teammate behind the ball is already an option).
3. THE SCORE. Q_floor = -L(actor location): the value of an unforced turnover right here (so a pass to nowhere that leaves an opponent one-on-one scores 0, and a turnover in the opponent box has a much higher floor than one at the edge of our own box).
   Q_best = max Q over the options above (including the chosen one); Q_chosen = Q of the option matching the action taken (the target of a failed pass is where the ball stopped; flagged).
   Score = 100 * (Q_chosen - Q_floor) / (Q_best - Q_floor), rounded to an integer; stakes = Q_best - Q_floor.
   LOW STAKES: stakes < F (F = 0.03 xG; 0.05 when the chosen or best option is a pass longer than 15 m; DQ-v12b) -> the score is shown shrunk toward 50:  50 + (score - 50) * stakes / (stakes + F), and flagged.
   OFFSIDE DOUBT: Q_best is computed from certain options only (headline) and from certain + doubtful options (reported next to it).
   UNCERTAINTY BAND: 100 perturbations per decision (every visible player N(0, 0.8 m); pass value scale LogNormal(0, 0.09); shot value scale LogNormal(0, 0.10); carry/dribble value scale LogNormal(0, sigma_c), sigma_c = between-competition spread of the P_keep * V_pos calibration measured in step 5; sizes from DQ-v12b / movement_noise.py); band = 10th to 90th percentile.
4. NEW MODELS, GATES ON TEST COMPETITIONS (a failed gate removes that option class from v1 and is reported):
   L (opponent part and own part): pooled predicted/observed in [0.85, 1.15] and fractional log-likelihood gain over the constant with a match-bootstrap 95% CI excluding 0 (positives are rare: wide intervals are expected and reported).
   P_keep (carries) and P_dribble: calibration slope in [0.8, 1.2], predicted/observed in [0.9, 1.1], AUC >= 0.60; if AUC < 0.60 the carry/dribble options are dropped from v1 and reported as "not supported".
   V_pos (value of having the ball at a position with these defenders): predicted/observed in [0.85, 1.15], slope in [0.8, 1.2].
   Between-competition spread tau of each model (leave-one-competition-out, DQ-v7 method) is reported and used for the noise sizes.
5. VALIDATION GATES (v1 numbers are published only if these pass; otherwise the score is reported as a diagnostic):
   G-known: among test decisions with stakes >= F, split by terciles of Q_best (situations of comparable promise); inside each tercile the mean REALISED net xG (own xG - opponent xG in the next 10 s) of the top score tercile must exceed that of the bottom score tercile, with a match-bootstrap 95% CI excluding 0 in at least 2 of the 3 strata and the direction right in all 3.
   G-anchor (unit tests, tests/): a shot from a situation worth 1 xG scores >= 95; a pass to nowhere that leaves an opponent one-on-one scores <= 5; the same turnover scores higher in the opponent box than at the edge of our own box; the score of the best option is 100.
   G-stability: among decisions with stakes >= F, median band width (90th - 10th percentile) <= 25 points and >= 70% of decisions keep their score tercile in >= 80% of the perturbations.
   G-visibility (descriptive): hiding a random half of the visible teammates (never the chosen target) changes the score by a median of <= 10 points; reported by number of visible teammates.
   G-curse (diagnostic): among decisions where the chosen option is also the modelled best, realised / predicted Q_best; if outside [0.8, 1.25] the output flags "optimistic best option"; a statistical correction is a v2 item.
   G-expert: NOT part of v1. The expert-panel pilot (reports/dq_expert_protocol.md) is the only external validation; until it is run the score is labelled "model-based, not validated by experts".
6. OUTPUTS. research/dq/dscore/ (module), a Decision Score table for the three stored matches (3895067, 3773526, 3749346) and for the test competitions (CSV, no player names), a report section, a plain-language summary.
   Shared helpers: research/dq/dscore/core.py replaces the copies in eval_v5 ... eval_v12b and match_review.py; regression check = the three stored match reviews are reproduced identically (done in Phase 0, see below).
7. LIMITS STATED IN ADVANCE. Carry and dribble options are hypothetical (we do not see which runs were available); no body orientation, velocities or intentions; only visible players count; the best option is a maximum over noisy values (winner's curse; diagnostic only in v1);
   the 0-100 scale is relative to the modelled options of the same situation, plus a situation-dependent turnover floor; a high score does not mean the player's intention was good; no player-level aggregation.
PHASE 0 RESULT (written after the refactor): research/dq/dscore/core.py created; research/cases/match_review.py now uses it; regenerated outputs for matches 3895067, 3773526 and 3749346 are IDENTICAL to the stored ones; tests/test_dscore_core.py adds 6 unit tests; full suite 139 passed.

AMENDMENT A (user clarification), 2026-11-15T12:25:50Z. Written before any model of this run was fitted (the data download was still running; Phase 0 only had refactored code).
The Decision Score is an ABSOLUTE scale over GENERAL decisions, not a normalisation by the best and worst option of the same moment. In a given situation there may be no option that scores 0 or 100; what we get is a score for EVERY option the player could have taken, and the gap between the option taken and the best option.
 WITHDRAWN: section 3's "Score = 100 * (Q_chosen - Q_floor) / (Q_best - Q_floor)" and the low-stakes shrinkage toward 50.
 REPLACEMENT. For every option a (shot, each visible teammate, each carry, the dribble, and the unforced turnover Q = -L(actor location)):
   Score(a) = 50 + 50 * min(Q, Q_hi) / Q_hi   for Q >= 0;      Score(a) = 50 - 50 * min(-Q, Q_lo) / Q_lo   for Q < 0;    rounded to an integer in 0-100.
   Q_hi = 0.70 xG (the user's real-world top: "a 1 xG shot, or above 0.7"; Q >= 0.70 scores 100). Q_lo = the 99.9th percentile of the realised turnover danger (opponent xG minus own xG in the next 10 s after a loss of the ball at x >= 50) in the TRAINING competitions; computed in step 2 and frozen before any scoring.
   50 means an action that changes nothing in expectation. An unforced turnover is just another option, with its situation-dependent score (L answers "not all turnovers are equal": a turnover at the edge of our box scores far lower than one in the opponent box).
 PER DECISION the output is: the score of every option, Score_chosen, Score_best (max over options), Score_worst, and  gap = Score_best - Score_chosen  (points) together with gap_xG = Q_best - Q_chosen (expected goals).
   Verdict tiers on gap_xG with the data-based floors of DQ-v12b (0.03 xG; 0.05 when the best or chosen option is a pass over 15 m): below the floor = "no clear difference from the best option"; the ratio rule of the shot-vs-pass verdict is kept when comparing a pass with a shot.
   The uncertainty band (perturbations) is computed for Score_chosen, Score_best and the gap. A second reference column "rarity" gives the percentile of Q among all general decisions of the training competitions.
 CHANGED GATES (replace G-known; the others are unchanged):
   G-calibration: pooled predicted/observed Q of the CHOSEN option in [0.85, 1.15] on the test competitions; and within deciles of Score_chosen the mean realised net xG (own minus opponent xG in the next 10 s) rises with the decile (Spearman >= 0.9 across the deciles; reported with a match-bootstrap interval).
   G-gap: inside terciles of Q_best (situations of comparable promise), decisions in the top tercile of gap_xG realise LESS net xG than those in the bottom gap tercile: direction right in all 3 strata and a match-bootstrap 95% CI excluding 0 in at least 2.
   G-anchor: a shot from a situation worth 1 xG scores 100 and gap 0 against any other option; a pass to nowhere that leaves an opponent one-on-one scores <= 10 and has the largest gap in its situation; the same unforced turnover scores higher in the opponent box than at the edge of our own box; the best option's gap is 0.
