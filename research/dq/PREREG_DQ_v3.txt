DQ-v3 PRE-REGISTRATION (options 2 and 1 after the v2 Metrica failure). Written 2026-11-13T11:39:40Z, before re-downloading or fitting.
Both options were chosen after seeing v2's Metrica slope 0.798 [0.72, 0.88]; they are post-hoc remedies, labelled as such.
Data, scope, step definition, covariates and fitting: identical to v2 (PREREG_DQ_v2.txt), except as stated.

OPTION 2 (visibility hypothesis): train the hazard only on what the camera saw.
 For each StatsBomb pass, keep its steps from the start up to (not including) the first step whose segment midpoint is outside the 360 visible area.
 If the pass leaves the visible area before it ends, it is CENSORED at that point (no event). A pass whose first step is out of view contributes nothing.
 The covariate in_view is dropped (constant). Assumption: leaving the visible area is independent of the hazard given covariates.
 Gate (same as v2): cross-competition both directions on in-view test rows (LL gain CI > 0, slope 0.8-1.2, O/E 0.85-1.15, tau <= 0.20);
 Metrica transfer (fitted on both competitions, all Metrica steps): pooled slope in [0.8, 1.2] and O/E in [0.85, 1.15], compared UNROUNDED.
 Prediction made in advance: if the visibility explanation is right, the Metrica slope should move toward 1 relative to v2 (0.798).

OPTION 1 (Metrica recalibration, leave-one-game-out): applied to the option-2 model if it fails the Metrica gate, otherwise reported as secondary;
 and in either case also applied to the v2 model for comparison.
 For each held-out Metrica game g: fit logit(h_cal) = a + b*logit(h) on the step rows of the other two games; apply to game g.
 Gate: pooled out-of-fold slope in [0.8, 1.2] and pooled O/E in [0.85, 1.15]; per-game slope and O/E reported (3 games, 268 events: wide intervals).
 Caveat stated in advance: recalibration uses Metrica labels from the other two games, i.e. it is a per-data-source calibration that would need re-estimation on any new tracking source.
