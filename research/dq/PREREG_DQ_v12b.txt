DQ-v12b PRE-REGISTRATION (addendum to PREREG_DQ_v12.txt): the same floor calibration with the noise sizes taken from data. Written 2026-11-15T11:52:32Z, before running it.
Noise sizes (replacing the assumptions of v12): position noise = per-axis sd of the displacement of a player within 25 m of the ball during the flight of a pass, measured on Metrica tracking (movement_noise_out.txt): 0.80 m for 0.4 s (short pass; PRIMARY), 1.98 m for 1.0 s (long pass; SENSITIVITY).
 Pass-value scale noise: LogNormal(0, 0.09), from the between-competition spread of the chain value (tau = 0.087, DQ-v7). Shot-value scale noise: LogNormal(0, 0.10), from the between-competition spread of xG predicted/observed goals (0.83-1.15, DQ-v8).
Everything else is unchanged from v12: same 300-shot sample (seed 0), 150 perturbations, bins of the conservative margin c, FLOOR* = lower edge of the first bin whose mean stability is >= 0.90 and stays >= 0.90 above; "clearly" = ratio rule AND margin >= FLOOR*; "leans" = margin >= 0.5 * FLOOR*.
If the primary and the sensitivity scenario give different floors, both are reported and the larger is used for passes longer than about 15 m (flight over 0.7 s), the primary otherwise; match reviews use the primary and mention the other.
Limit stated in advance: the displacement of a player is not the same as an error in the model's input (a player who moves is a different situation, not a noisy measurement), so this noise represents how stale the freeze frame is for a hypothetical pass; the measurement error of the frames themselves (which we cannot measure) is not included.
